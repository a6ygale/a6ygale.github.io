About the researcher

A physics foundation, electrical-engineering practice, and a continuing interest in shaping electromagnetic fields.

PHYSICS → ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING → COMPUTATIONAL EM

Curiosity became a way of working.

My path into electromagnetic systems began with physics: learning to derive models, question assumptions, and connect mathematical descriptions to observable behavior. Research in microscopy introduced me to experimental control and image processing; electrical engineering expanded that foundation into RF systems and propagation.

Today, I am especially interested in the computational side of electromagnetic design — how geometry, phase, material response, and optimization can create new capabilities in sensing and wireless communication.

This portfolio is a working record of that journey: projects, derivations, experiments, and questions I want to pursue more deeply.
